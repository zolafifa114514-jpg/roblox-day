WHY SCHOOLS USE IT

Learning first.
Making makes it stick.

Build Day turns an engaging creative project into a clear learning sequence. Students define a goal, divide responsibilities, test their decisions, respond to feedback, and explain their choices.

01 / LEARNING OUTCOMES

What students
practice.

Teachers do not need to promise that every student becomes a game developer. The goal is a supported, visible practice of transferable skills.

Problem solving

02

03

05 / SCHOOL FAQ

Questions a
principal asks.

Here are the practical details to review before bringing Build Day to a class or club.

Will this disrupt the school day? +

It is planned as a structured workshop with a published run-of-show. Schools can use a full day, a club block, or adapt the phases to their timetable.

Does a teacher need to know Roblox Studio? +

No. The teacher guide and beginner activities provide the structure. A facilitator can model the tool basics while the teacher supports teams and discussion.

What does the school need? +

Students need access to Roblox Studio and a Roblox account. The school provides supervised devices, a teacher or facilitator, and time for the showcase.

How much does it cost? +

Build Day is designed as a free program. Request the current school information packet for availability, implementation details, and any local requirements.
