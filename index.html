06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We’ve got you.

Start small. Ask questions. Make something you’re proud of.

Do I need to know how to code? +

Nope. Build Day starts at the beginning with beginner-friendly activities. You’ll learn by trying things out.

Who can participate? +

Roblox players ages 10+ can join with a parent or guardian’s okay. You can build by yourself or with a small team.

What do I need before I start? +

A Roblox account, access to Roblox Studio, and one idea you’re excited to explore.

Is Build Day really free? +

Yes. The online challenge, activities, showcase, and digital badge are all free.
